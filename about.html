About

A Ghana-first travel platform, built for ordinary travellers.

TripNest exists because planning a trip in Ghana usually means stitching together screenshots, word-of-mouth and half-finished notes. This is an attempt to do it properly in one place.

Why we built it

Ghana has extraordinary places: castles that carry the weight of the Atlantic world, rainforest canopies, crater lakes, stilt villages, northern savannah and a coastline that runs for hundreds of kilometres. Most of it is badly served by generic travel sites.

TripNest treats Ghanaian destinations as worth a proper guide: what the place is, why it matters, what you can do there, when to go, where to stay and what to think about before you arrive. Then it gives you tools to turn that reading into an actual plan.

What is in the platform

17 published destination guides, 28 attractions and experiences — including 11 flagged as hidden gems — and 16 published accommodation listings across 8 regions.

Travellers get saved places, dated itineraries with per-stop estimates and a budget summary in Ghana cedis. Providers submit listings which administrators review before publication.

Practical notes, honestly

  • · Seasonal guidance is general, not a forecast.
  • · Fares, timetables and entry fees are not published as facts.
  • · Accommodation prices are indicative and confirmed by providers.
  • · Some listing photographs are labelled area illustrations.

Editorial, not promotional

Guides are written to explain a place and its context — honest about travel times, seasons, and what to expect.

Accessible by default

Contrast-checked colour, keyboard navigation, real form labels, reduced-motion support and responsive layouts.

Honest about data

Content is flagged as seed or verified. Photography is licensed and credited. Nothing pretends to be a live booking system.
