Frequently asked

Can I book accommodation through TripNest?
No. TripNest does not take bookings or payments. Each listing links to the provider's phone, WhatsApp or email so you can confirm directly.
Why are prices shown if they are not bookable?
Nightly rates help you judge whether a place fits your budget. They are indicative and confirmed by the provider.
How do I list a property?
Create a provider account, then use the provider dashboard to add the property. An administrator reviews it before it goes live.

Where we are

TripNest is built and maintained in Kumasi, Ashanti Region, Ghana. We do not operate a walk-in office for travellers — accommodation and transport questions should go to the providers themselves.
