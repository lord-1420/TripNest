Privacy

What TripNest stores, why, and how you can remove it. Written to match what this browser-based application actually does.

Local browser storage

This version of TripNest runs entirely in your browser using HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript. Accounts, saved places, trips, itineraries, budgets, packing checklists, and provider listings are saved in your browser's localStorage on this device — nothing is transmitted to a remote server.

Maps and imagery

Interactive maps use Leaflet (OpenStreetMap tiles) and MapLibre GL (Esri World Imagery tiles). When a map loads, your browser requests map tiles directly from those providers.

Your choices

You can delete any trip at any time from the trip planner, or click Reset demo data inside the dashboard sidebar (or clear site data in your browser) to wipe all stored data immediately.
