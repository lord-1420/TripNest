Terms of use

Plain-language terms for using TripNest. This build is provided for evaluation and is not a commercial booking service.

1. What TripNest is

TripNest is a travel discovery and planning platform focused on Ghana. It publishes editorial destination guides, attraction records and accommodation listings submitted by providers, and offers tools for saving places, building itineraries and estimating costs in cedis.

2. What TripNest is not

TripNest is not a travel agent, tour operator or booking platform. We do not take payments, hold reservations or guarantee availability, prices or opening hours. Any agreement you reach with a provider is directly between you and them.

3. Content accuracy

Destination and attraction content in this build is seed content awaiting editorial verification. Travel information changes — always confirm current details locally before you travel.
