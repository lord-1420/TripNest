Travel essentials

Travel smarter in Ghana.

Practical local guidance for first-time visitors, students, and solo travellers.

Best times to visit

Dry season runs roughly November to March in the south, with heavier rains May–July. The north is hottest before the rains arrive.

Seasonal destinations →

Getting around

Intercity buses and trotros cover Ghana affordably. For national parks and remote villages, hiring a driver for the day is usually best.

Open 3D map →

Money & connectivity

Mobile Money (MoMo) is everywhere and cards work in hotels and larger spots. Carry cash cedis for markets and local transport.

Practical guide →

Health & preparation

Check malaria guidance for your regions, stick to bottled or filtered water, and pack basic rehydration salts and sunscreen.
